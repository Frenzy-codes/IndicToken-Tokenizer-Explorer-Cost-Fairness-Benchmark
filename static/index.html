Addressing the Disparity: Technical Mitigations

Research Consensus

The token tax is not an inherent linguistic property of Hindi or Marathi—it is an artifact of pretraining corpus imbalance. When tokenizer training sets are 90%+ English, frequent Devanagari character triples never meet merge frequency thresholds, forcing byte fallbacks.

  • Vocabulary Allocation: Giving Indic scripts reserved vocabulary quotas (as seen in models like Sarvam AI's OpenHathi and Llama-3-Indic).
  • Byte-level Unigram Models: SentencePiece configurations that prioritize character frequency per script rather than global corpus frequency.
  • Character-level & Byte-latent architectures: Models that operate without explicit tokenization (e.g. MambaByte, MegaByte) naturally eliminate the tax.
